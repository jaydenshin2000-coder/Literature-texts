Did—ever—the white coats understand the scene I saw? Movements captured within a blink; Nothing was alive in my pictures. A tooth shivers while I stretch my tongue It murmurs a phrase like a code in a battle: “A dye, pipe and a dime!” Then it loudly laughs, mocking its owner In front of me; Machines with plastic capsules Some toys inhale the thin air inside At my left pocket; A lighter, I got from elsewhere, clatters as my nails tap its fueled body “A dye, pipe and a dime. That's what I'm supposed to do!” Dangling tooth repeats its words— Making me believe it's not spoken from a fool Crack! As my hand fulfills its first claim, Rouge spills out between my dry lips, soaking the half-tattered blue garments, and dyeing the skin I wore for this night My hand then grabs the lighter in pocket The red—busy while smearing to others— darkens its color, turning into an oil So, my name smells like the red and black This hand-made geyser between two teeth, erupts petroleum with all its might! The burning soul is delighted by the view As they meet, a mouthful of steam excites the two My left hand; a used lighter for a ‘pipe’, Right hand; a freak, not even once blemished My right stuffs it into the narrow hole, sculpting its body in right's own right. The square machine drills its own prison One escapes from everlasting despair; A green capsule imitates a real medicine, so I open its guts, to see the truthful ones Buses of RGB—Are you my new doctors down the street? They play a mystery directed by Hitchcock; Vehicles move on their own, riding lights although inside, all the people were dead. So I put those toys inside my mouth, envying the vigor made from steel and plastic! The past is now, and the future is then. Behind me; between a frame of the pillars, Colors start to dance in backwards, As if the city wants its puberty again My left; A passerby walking on the street who was checking the lights, a few minutes ago Now, as her stiff body became colored lines, dives into the stripes, trying to be swallowed Again—my right; Cars drift in cold air Buildings rattle their windows, calling my bloody name As the lips answer, more red is squeezed out, then my head spins, in the mixture of scenes Lightheaded, so all dead mysteries go alive I gulp; swallowing the taste of pungent triumph I laugh; mocking my attitude of past doubts Tumbled, the road comes near as my head falls ahead So a dime, pipe, and a dye. Not a fool—was one's wisdom indeed!
